what joice does

more than a journal. a companion.

Everything in Joice is built around one idea: talking things out is the most natural way to understand yourself.

talk, don't type

Just speak naturally — like you're talking to a close friend. No blank pages, no writer's block. Joice responds in real time with genuine curiosity.

discover patterns

Joice surfaces recurring themes in your life — career, relationships, stress — so you can see the bigger picture of what's on your mind.

private & secure

Your journal is yours. Encrypted storage, no data selling, and you can delete everything at any time. Your thoughts stay between you and Joice.

automatic transcripts

Every conversation is transcribed and searchable. Look back on any entry, see your themes evolve, and track your journey over time.

build the habit

Streaks, gentle reminders, and a beautiful calendar of your journaling history. Joice helps you show up for yourself consistently.

weekly reflections

Every week, Joice writes you a warm reflection on what you talked about — patterns you might have missed, growth you might not have noticed.
